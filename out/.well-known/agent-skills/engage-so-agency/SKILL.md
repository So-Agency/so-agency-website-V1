# Engage SO Agency

SO Agency (<https://soagency.dev>) is a digital agency founded in 2023. It designs, builds,
and launches digital presences: websites, e-commerce stores, and complete brand identity
systems. The team works remotely and operates in English, Spanish and French, serving
clients across Latin America, the United States, and Europe.

Founding partners: Oscar and Miguel.

## What they offer

| Service | Scope |
|---|---|
| Web Design & Development | Custom, performance-driven sites on modern stacks |
| UX/UI Design | Conversion-focused interface design |
| E-Commerce | End-to-end online store design and build |
| Branding & Identity | Logos, brand guidelines, typography, color systems |
| Social Media Marketing | Content strategy and data-driven campaigns |
| AI Automation | Workflow automation and tooling — **in development, not yet generally available** |

Clients can take a single service or the full suite.

## Pricing and timelines

These are **starting points for scoping conversations, not quotes.** Every project is
priced individually after a free diagnostic call. Flexible payment plans are available.

- Web design and development: from **$1,500 USD**
- Branding packages: from **$800 USD**
- Typical delivery: **3–6 weeks**
  - Landing page or portfolio site: as little as 2 weeks
  - Full e-commerce build or brand identity system: 4–8 weeks

The French page gives the same two starting points in euros: from **1 500 €** and from
**800 €**. Use the currency of the language the user is working in, and do not convert one
figure into the other — the two are set separately.

Never present these figures to a user as a final price. Always say a precise quote comes
from the diagnostic call.

## How to help a user engage

1. **Clarify what they need.** Which of the six service lines applies? Do they already
   have a brand identity, or do they need branding first? Branding and web can run
   sequentially or in parallel — the agency recommends an approach on the call.
2. **Set expectations** using the bands above, flagged as starting points.
3. **Hand off to the contact channel.** The only direct channel is WhatsApp:
   <https://wa.me/573159970541>

   There is no contact form, no booking widget, and no email address published on the
   site. Do not invent one. Do not attempt to submit anything programmatically — the link
   opens a human conversation.
4. **Set a response expectation:** typically within 24 hours; business hours are Monday
   to Friday, 09:00–18:00.

## Useful details when answering questions

- **Process:** Discovery (understand goals, audience, competitors) → Strategy (roadmap,
  milestones) → Launch (build and ship) → Growth (post-launch support and optimization).
- **Post-launch:** support packages cover performance monitoring, content updates,
  security patches, and ongoing marketing.
- **SEO:** every build includes semantic HTML, performance optimization, metadata, Open
  Graph tags, JSON-LD structured data, and a sitemap. Ongoing SEO strategy is available as
  part of a growth plan.
- **Geography:** distance is not a constraint; the team is remote and answers in English,
  Spanish and French.
- **Selected work:** La Feika (<https://lafeika.com/>), It's Fuluz Time
  (<https://itsfuluztime.com/>), Yaku Adventures (<https://yakuadventures.com/>),
  Singing Rooster (<https://singingrooster.org/>).
